01 · FRONTEND PROMPTING & SPOTLIGHT

Message your agent straight from the page

Stop pasting 4 MB Retina screenshots or switching back to your editor. Alt + Click any component (or type directly inside the HUD “Make this CTA indigo”) with ~300 tokens of exact context. In return, your agent can ask questions or live-preview CSS via its Spotlight Popin before touching disk.

Interactive Popin & direct targeting Click “Alt+Click Target” to simulate a pin, or run the full agent loop in one click
02 · LOCAL-FIRST · LIVE WATCH & HANDOVER

Your agent sees the real page — without locking you out

No browser extension to maintain, no sandbox network popups: everything syncs over local .jolt/ files and MCP. During an active watch session (jolt-mcp listen), the HUD enters LIVE mode — click Stop anytime to end listening and take back IDE control.

.jolt/digest.json Live runtime telemetry
